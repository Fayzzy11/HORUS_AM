Join the research team.

Horus is a student research house. From January 2027 we run our strategy live and publish it every month, and we are looking for students who want to build it with us.

Why Horus

Learn asset management by doing it.

I

Real process

Work on a rules-based strategy, from data to factsheet, the way an asset manager does.

II

Your name on the research

Every research note is signed by its authors and published on this site.

III

A track record to show

A live paper-trading track record from 2027, ideal for internship and job interviews.

Open roles

Four ways to contribute.

Student role · Paris or remote · about 3 h a week

Junior Research Analyst

Test new factors (quality, low volatility), extend the universe to European small caps, write one research note a quarter.

We look for: curiosity for academic finance, Excel, clear writing.

Student role · Paris or remote · about 3 h a week

Quant & Data

Update the monthly data, run the backtest and the Carhart checks in Python, automate the factsheet.

We look for: Python (pandas, numpy), rigour, taste for automation.

Student role · Paris or remote · about 3 h a week

Macro & Markets

Write the monthly market outlook and the comment of the factsheet, follow central banks and rates.

We look for: passion for markets, a daily reading habit, concise English.

Student role · Paris or remote · about 3 h a week

Communication & Design

Run the newsletter, the website and our LinkedIn page, design the charts of the monthly letter.

We look for: a sense of design, social media, interest in finance.
