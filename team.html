How we work

Governance of a small investment committee.

I

Rules first

The process is written before the data is looked at. No discretionary override.

II

Four eyes

Every number is computed twice, in Excel and in Python, and must match.

III

One vote each

Changes to the strategy need a majority of the committee and a new out-of-sample test.

IV

Full disclosure

What we tested and rejected is published, not hidden.
